Why a dollar figure matters more than a list

A list of 40 approvals is noise. "$12,340, signed 3 years ago, still valid" is a decision.

Approval exploits do not need your seed phrase, your password, or your hardware wallet. They only need the permission you already granted and forgot about. Quantifying it is the fastest way to make it real.

What it cannot tell you — read this

Is the dollar figure exact?

No. It is a floor, not a ceiling. We only price tokens that have a public market price. Tokens without a price are listed separately as unpriced, and they are usually the obscure ones — which are often the dangerous ones. Treat the number as "at least this much".

Which chains are covered?

ERC-20 approvals on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon and Base. NFT approvals on all of those except Base, where the data provider does not support it yet.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

Not to scan. Scanning is read-only and only needs a public address. You only connect a wallet if you decide to revoke something, and the transaction is signed by you in your own wallet — this page never sees a key.

Does revoking cost money?

Yes, one gas fee per revocation. This page does not charge anything and does not take a cut. "Batch" here means a queue: you pick a group — all high-risk, all unlimited, everything older than two years — and we walk through it one by one. Each one still needs your own wallet confirmation, because a bundled multicall cannot revoke on your behalf.

Does it catch Permit2 or signed permits?

No, and you should know that. Permit2 allowances and EIP-2612 permits are not stored as token allowances, so public approval APIs do not return them. If you have signed "Permit" popups in Uniswap-style apps, they are not in this report.

Does it remember my last check?

Only inside your own browser. The record never leaves the device and there is no account. When you scan again you get one line telling you how many approvals appeared and how many are gone since last time.

Can this recover stolen funds?

No. Nothing can. This is prevention. If your seed phrase is compromised and a sweeper bot drains any ETH you send in, revoking approvals will not help — abandon that wallet.

Why is it free?

Because the expensive part is reading public on-chain data, and we do that through a third-party security API. No account, no ads, no data collection. If it becomes expensive to run at scale, we will say so.

Is the Chinese version different?
